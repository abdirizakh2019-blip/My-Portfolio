Qiimaha wuxuu kuxiran yahay nooca iyo baaxadda mashruuca. Website fudud wuxuu bilaabmaa qiimo jaban, halka mashruucyada waaweyn ee leh nidaam (system) ay u baahan yihiin qiimo dheeraad ah. Buuxi foomka WhatsApp si aad u hesho qiyaas sax ah oo dhamaystiran.

Website fudud waxay qaadan kartaa 3-7 maalmood, halka mashruucyo waaweyn sida nidaam maamul (school/business management system) ay qaadan karaan 2-4 toddobaad iyadoo ku xiran baaxadda shaqada.

Labadaba way suurtagal tahay. Haddii aad horay u haysatid domain iyo hosting waan kaa caawin karaa inaan website-ka kor ka dhigo (deploy). Haddii aadan haysan, waan kuu soo jeedin karaa ikhtiyaaro ku habboon baahidaada iyo lacagtaada.

Haa, dhammaan website-yada waxaan u sameeyaa si dib loogu cusboonaysiin karo. Waxaan sidoo kale bixiyaa adeeg maintenance ah oo joogto ah si website-kaagu had iyo jeer u shaqeeyo si fiican.

Waxaan sameeyaa labadaba — website iyo mobile application (Android & iOS). Haddii aad u baahan tahay labadaba, waan ku samayn karaa qorshe isku xidhan oo ay ku shaqeeyaan hal database.

Caadi ahaan waxaan u qaybiyaa lacagta laba qayb: hormarin (deposit) marka mashruuca la bilaabo, iyo inta soo hartay marka mashruuca la dhammeeyo oo la hubiyo in wax walba u shaqeeyaan si fiican.

Haa, waxaan bixiyaa taageero kadib-dhammaystir ah si loo xaliyo wax kasta oo dhibaato ah oo soo baxa, iyo sidoo kale kobcinta website-ka mustaqbalka haddii loo baahdo.
